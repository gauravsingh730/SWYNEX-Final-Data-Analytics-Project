# SWYNEX-Final-Data-Analytics-Project

# Final Project Report: Global Retail Performance Dashboard

## Project Overview
The objective of this project is to analyze Global Superstore sales data and identify important trends and patterns in sales, profit, customers, products, regions, and discounts. The analysis aims to help understand overall business performance, identify highly profitable and underperforming areas, and provide useful insights for better business decision-making.

## Dataset Information
The underlying dataset consists of comprehensive retail transaction records:
* **Total Records:** 51,290 transactions
* **Total Features:** 26 columns
* **Timespan:** 4 Years (2011–2014)
* **Key Dimensions:** Order Date, Customer Segment, Region, Market, Category, Sub-Category
* **Key Metrics:** Sales, Quantity, Discount, Profit, Shipping Cost

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
* **Total Sales:** $12,642,501.91
* **Total Profits:** $1,467,457.29

## Visualizations Included
The dashboard features the following targeted visualizations:
* **Sales & Profit Trend by Year:** Demonstrates yearly growth from 2011 to 2014.
* **Top 5 Sales By Region:** Highlights the highest-grossing geographical regions, led by the Central region ($2,822,302.52).
* **Total Profit by Market:** Compares profitability across global markets like APAC, EU, US, LATAM, Africa, EMEA, and Canada.
* **Top 10 Most Profitable Sub-Categories:** Identifies high-margin product groups, led by Copiers and Phones.
* **Profit By Category:** Breaks down margins across Technology ($663,778.73), Office Supplies ($518,473.83), and Furniture ($285,204.72).

## Key Business Insights
* **Consistent Year-Over-Year Growth:** The company has seen sustained upward momentum, with total sales nearly doubling from $2.25 million in 2011 to $4.29 million in 2014, while maintaining proportional profitability.
* **APAC is the Most Lucrative Market:** The Asia-Pacific (APAC) market is the dominant driver of global revenue, generating the highest sales ($3.58 million) and contributing the largest share of net profit ($436K).
* **Technology Drives High Profits:** Technology is the most profitable category globally, generating approximately $663K in profit, heavily driven by "Copiers" ($258K) and "Phones" ($216K).
* **Office Supplies Yield Efficient Margins:** While Technology generates the most total revenue, Office Supplies operate on highly efficient profit margins, generating $518K in profit from just $3.78 million in sales.
* **Underperforming Regions Require Attention:** The Canadian market is the weakest contributor to overall sales and profit globally, and regions like EMEA and Africa contribute minimally to the global profit pool. This suggests a need to re-evaluate localized pricing, product offerings, or resource allocation in these areas.
* **"Central" and "South" are the Top Regions:** From a granular geographic perspective, the Central and South regions generate the highest sales volume compared to other global territories[cite: 3].
